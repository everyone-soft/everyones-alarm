Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2025

Welcome to Everyone's Alarm. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy will inform you about how we handle your personal information when you use our app and tell you about your privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

Everyone's Alarm is designed to be privacy-focused. We collect minimal data necessary for the app to function:

  • Alarm Data: We store the alarm information you create (time, date, title, category, repeat settings) locally on your device.
  • App Usage Data: Basic information about how you use the app may be collected anonymously to improve the app experience.
  • Device Permissions: We request notification permissions to deliver your alarms.

We do not collect or store any personal identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of the app (setting and triggering alarms)
  • Improve and optimize the app experience
  • Respond to your requests or questions

3. Data Storage

All alarm data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms. We do not transmit your alarm data to any external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may use Apple's analytics services to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how users interact with our app and identify areas for improvement. This data is not linked to your identity.

5. Your Rights

Since we store your data locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can:

  • Delete any alarm data by removing alarms in the app
  • Clear all data by uninstalling the app
  • Control notification permissions through your device settings

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages and does not collect personal information from children or anyone else.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
